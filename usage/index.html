    # System User Guide

    This guide explains how to use the **FSL Learn** platform according to your assigned user role: **Student (Learner)**, **Teacher**, or **Principal**.

    Every feature described below is implemented in the platform's user interface. Please follow the numbered instructions and exact button names to complete each task.

    ## User Roles and Permissions

    The system enforces strict role-based access control. Review the permissions assigned to each account type:

    | User Role | Dashboard URL | Accessible Navigation Items | Primary Responsibilities |

    | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

    | **Student** | student.html | **FSL Lessons**, **My Classroom**, **Profile** | Browse lessons across Alphabet, Basic, and Intermediate levels; watch sign demonstration GIFs; mark lessons complete; take multiple-choice quizzes; track personal scores. |

    | **Teacher** | teacher.html | **FSL Lessons**, **Classrooms**, **Sign and Spell**, **Profile** | Create and manage classrooms; enroll new and existing students; track individual and class progress metrics; broadcast live sign demonstrations via camera recognition. |

    | **Principal** | teacher.html | **Report**, **Code Generator**, **Profile** | Generate and expire 16-character Teacher Registration Codes; review school-wide learning progress; export official Teachers, Students, and Progress reports to PDF/CSV. |

    Note

    Students can belong to only one classroom at a time. If a student transfers to a new classroom, their previous teacher must remove them before the new teacher can enroll them.

    ## Accessing the Platform and Logging In

    All users access the application through the centralized sign-in interface. Follow these steps:

    1. Open your web browser and navigate to the application login page: http://localhost/fsl\_learn/frontend/login.html

    2. The login screen presents two role tabs: **Student** and **Teacher**:

    - To log in as a student, click the **Student** tab.

    - To log in as a teacher or principal, click the **Teacher** tab.

    3. Enter your assigned **Username** and **Password**.

    4. Click the **Login** (or **Sign In**) button.

    5. Upon successful authentication, students are directed to student.html, while teachers and principals are routed to teacher.html.

    ## Teacher Account Registration

    New teachers must register an account using a valid **Teacher Registration Code** generated by the School Principal:

    1. Navigate to the login page (login.html) and select the **Teacher** tab.

    2. In the registration form (or by toggling to the register view), complete the required fields:

    - **First Name** and **Last Name** (combined into Full Name)

    - **Username** (3 to 30 characters; letters, numbers, underscores, dots, or hyphens)

    - **Email Address** (optional, for contact records)

    - **Password** (minimum of 6 characters)

    - **Confirm Password** (must match password exactly)

    - **Teacher Registration Code** (16-character code in the format FSL-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX)

    3. Click the **Create Teacher Account** button.

    4. The system validates the code against the database. If the code is active and unredeemed, your teacher account is created immediately and you are logged in to the Teacher Dashboard.

    ## Teacher Classroom Management

    Teachers organize their learners into distinct classrooms.

    ### Creating a Classroom

    Follow these steps to establish a new classroom:

    1. Log in to the Teacher Dashboard (teacher.html).

    2. Click **Classrooms** in the left sidebar navigation (data-view="students").

    3. Click the **Create New Classroom** button (openCreateModal()).

    4. In the modal dialog:

    - Enter the **Classroom Name** (e.g., Grade 1 - Daisy).

    - Enter a brief **Description** (optional).

    5. Click **Create Classroom**.

    6. The system generates a unique 6-character alphanumeric **Classroom Code** (e.g., QPELNG) and displays the classroom in your directory.

    ### Managing Classroom Details

    Follow these steps to update or inspect a classroom:

    1. In the **Classrooms** list, click on any classroom card to open its detail view.

    2. To update the name or description, click the edit icon, update the fields, and click **Save Changes**.

    3. To view enrolled learners, scroll down to the **Classroom Learners** table.

    Warning

    Deleting a classroom is irreversible. Deleting a classroom permanently disassociates its enrolled students and deletes classroom-specific quiz results. Always ensure student records are backed up before removing a classroom.

    ## Enrolling and Managing Learners

    Teachers create student accounts directly inside their classrooms. Students do not self-register on the public landing page.

    ### Adding a New Student to a Classroom

    Follow these steps to create and enroll a new student account:

    1. In the Teacher Dashboard, select **Classrooms** and click your desired classroom.

    2. Click the **Add Learner** button to launch the student creation modal.

    3. Complete the learner profile details:

    - **Full Name** (e.g., Marian Marchan)

    - **Username** (e.g., marian10949)

    - **Password** (temporary password for the pupil, at least 6 characters)

    - **Age** (e.g., 10)

    - **Gender** (select Boy, Girl, Male, Female, or Other)

    4. Click **Save Learner**.

    5. The student is created in the database, assigned the student role, and enrolled into your classroom. Provide the username and temporary password to the student or their guardian.

    ### Enrolling an Existing Learner

    Follow these steps to link a student account to your classroom:

    1. If the pupil already has an account in the system and is not currently enrolled in any other classroom, open your classroom and click **Enroll Existing Learner**.

    2. Select or search for the learner by username or name.

    3. Click **Confirm Enrollment**. If the student is already enrolled in another classroom, an alert will notify you to resolve the transfer with the previous teacher first.

    ## Student Learning Experience

    Students use the **FSL Lessons** portal to study Filipino Sign Language vocabulary:

    1. Log in as a student on login.html.

    2. Click **FSL Lessons** (data-view="learn") in the left sidebar or bottom mobile bar.

    3. The screen presents three progressive learning tiers:

    - **Alphabet**: 26 letters of the FSL alphabet.

    - **Basic Vocabulary**: 15 categories (Numbers, Colors, Fruits, Vegetables, Animals, Food, etc.).

    - **Intermediate Vocabulary**: 9 categories (Occupations, Transportation, Emergencies, Community, etc.).

    4. Click on a level, then select a category card (e.g., Colors or Everyday Greetings).

    5. Click on an individual lesson card to open the **Lesson Viewer**:

    - Observe the high-definition animated sign demonstration GIF.

    - Read the English and Filipino vocabulary term.

    - Review the descriptive signing instructions and **Helpful Tips**.

    6. Practice the sign with your hand.

    7. Click the **Mark Lesson as Complete** button. The system updates your progress bar and awards completion credit.

    ## Taking Module Mastery Quizzes

    Each learning level and category includes multiple-choice quizzes to evaluate sign comprehension:

    1. Navigate to **FSL Lessons** and scroll to the bottom of the module or category to find the quiz banner (e.g., **Alphabet Mastery Quiz**).

    2. Click **Start Quiz**.

    3. Read the prompt and examine the displayed sign image or question term.

    4. Click one of the multiple-choice options.

    5. The animated **Feedback Tray** slides up from the bottom of the screen:

    - A green tray with a checkmark indicates a **Correct** response.

    - A red tray indicates an **Incorrect** response and highlights the correct sign.

    6. Click **Continue** to move to the next question.

    7. Upon answering all questions, the quiz completion summary appears, displaying your total points, total questions, and percentage score.

    8. Scores meeting or exceeding 70% earn passing status and are recorded in the database under quiz\_results.

    ## Teacher "Sign \& Spell" Live Alphabet Recognition

    Teachers can demonstrate and practice signs live in front of the class using the integrated AI computer vision tool:

    1. Log in to the Teacher Dashboard (teacher.html).

    2. Click **Sign and Spell** in the sidebar navigation (data-view="sign-spell").

    3. Click the **Start Camera** button. When prompted by your browser, click **Allow** to grant webcam access.

    4. Position yourself so your upper body and hands are visible inside the video frame.

    5. Raise your dominant hand facing the camera. The MediaPipe hand tracker draws landmark connections across your hand joints.

    6. Form an FSL alphabet letter (e.g., A, B, or C).

    7. The system transmits the 63 normalized coordinates to the local ML service (http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/recognize).

    8. The predicted letter and confidence score appear in real time on the live recognition display.

    9. To conclude the demonstration, click **Stop Camera**.

    ## Principal Code Generator

    The School Principal is the sole authority permitted to issue Teacher Registration Codes:

    1. Log in with the protected Principal account (username: principal).

    2. Click **Code Generator** in the sidebar navigation (data-view="code").

    3. In the security check modal, re-enter your **Principal Password** to confirm your identity.

    4. Select the code **Expiration Window**:

    - 24 Hours

    - 7 Days

    - 30 Days

    - Never Expire [TODO: confirm]

    5. Click **Generate Code**.

    6. The system creates a new 16-character code (e.g., FSL-3A9F1C2B-E04D7A6F) and adds it to the **Active Registration Codes** table.

    7. Click the **Copy Code** button next to the entry to copy it to your clipboard and deliver it to the onboarding teacher.

    ## Principal Reports \& School Analytics

    The Principal monitors school-wide learning metrics and generates printable documentation for school records:

    1. In the Principal Dashboard, click **Report** in the sidebar navigation (data-view="report").

    2. Select one of the three primary report types:

    - **Teachers Report**: List of registered teachers, assigned classrooms, and total enrolled pupils.

    - **Students Report**: Roster of students with completed lesson counts, average quiz scores, and last active dates.

    - **Overall Learning Progress Report**: School-wide breakdown across Alphabet, Basic, and Intermediate categories.

    3. Use the top filter controls to narrow data by **School Year** [TODO: confirm], **Grade Level**, or **Section**.

    4. To save an official copy, click **Export PDF**. The browser compiles the report using jsPDF and AutoTable and initiates an immediate file download.

    5. To export raw spreadsheet data, click **Export CSV**.

    ## Profile Management and Logging Out

    Users can update their personal profile information and terminate sessions securely.

    ### Updating Profile Details

    Follow these steps to modify your user account:

    1. Click the **Profile** button at the bottom of the sidebar.

    2. Update your **Full Name** or contact **Email**.

    3. To upload a personal picture, click **Upload Avatar**, select a JPEG or PNG image from your computer, and save.

    4. To change your password, enter your current password, type your new password, and click **Update Password**.

    ### Logging Out

    Follow these steps to end your session:

    1. Click the **Logout** button located in the sidebar or mobile menu.

    2. In the **Log Out?** confirmation modal, click **Log Out** to confirm.

    3. The platform clears session tokens and redirects you to login.html.

    For assistance troubleshooting common login or runtime errors, see the [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md).
